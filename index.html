⚠️ CARICOM Single Market & Economy (CSME) Expatriate Limitation

While the CSME grants Community Treatment (0% tariffs) to qualifying goods and free movement of skilled persons within participating member states, this right applies exclusively to CARICOM citizens holding a CSME Skills Certificate. United States citizens are classified as third-country nationals under the Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas.

🏛️ Central Bank & Foreign Exchange Controls

Belize pegs its currency at 2.00 BZD = 1.00 USD. To hold foreign currency for settling ocean freight lines, companies must apply to the Central Bank for a Foreign Currency Account (Form XCH9).

Tax Jurisdiction Dual-Requirements

Belize Tax Service (BTS / IRIS)

Assesses Business Tax on gross turnover (1.75% to 6.0%) rather than net income.

US Expatriate Tax (IRS Requirements)

US citizens face worldwide taxation. Active expats can use the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion for earned wages.

🗺️ Regional Trade Corridors & Execution Mechanics

Understand the physical routing, border transfers, and customs clearance procedures across the three main trade routes servicing Belize and Central America.

Corridor 1🚢

US-to-Belize Ocean Shipping

FCL/LCL ocean freight originating from Miami, Port Everglades, or Houston sailing to Belize.

•Carriers: King Ocean, Seaboard, Hyde Shipping.
•Clearance: ASYCUDA World via licensed declarant.
Primary Key: FMC NVOCC License + House Bill of Lading
Corridor 2🚚

Overland Mexico Transshipment

Overland highway transit moving through Mexico to the Chetumal and Belize border transfer points.

•Drayage Handoff: Mexican line-haul trailers unhitch in border zone.
•Local Delivery: Belizean towheads continue domestic transit.
Primary Key: Cross-border Drayage Contract & Santa Elena Post
Corridor 3🌴

Intra-CARICOM Trade Routes

Regional maritime feeder routes connecting Jamaica, Trinidad, Guyana, and Barbados to Belize.

•Tariffs: 0% Community Duty under Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas.
•Mandate: Verified CARICOM Certificate of Origin required.
Primary Key: Kingston / South Florida Hub Transshipment
